# Bajaj Electricals - Official 2026 Product Catalogues Hub

A modern, responsive, and high-performance product catalogue landing page designed specifically for **Bajaj Electricals**, featuring 4 official multi-page PDF catalogues, lead capture, and a password-protected **Admin Portal**.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Highlights
- **Executive Engineering Aesthetic**: Deep slate and obsidian backgrounds (`#060911`, `#0c121e`) with Bajaj electric blue/cyan accents (`#0084ff`, `#00d2ff`), copper wire orange, and switchgear gold.
- **3D Visual Booklet Covers**: Custom visual 3D booklet previews with realistic bindings, badges, and cover graphics.
- **Trust Strip**: Highlights Flame Retardant Copper Wires, EyeRelax™ LED technology, 10kA MCB breaking capacity, and Luxury Architectural Luminaires.
- **Live Search & Filter Bar**: Instant client-side filtering by keyword and category pills (*Wires & Cables, Consumer Lighting, MCB & Switchgear, Decorative Lighting*).
- **In-Browser Multi-Page PDF Viewer**: Instant modal preview with zoom and full-page controls.
- **Batch Download**: 1-click option to download all 4 catalogues.
- **Customer Inquiry & Lead Capture Form**: Captures Name, Work Email, Phone/WhatsApp, Company, City, Product requested, and custom project requirements.
- **🔒 Password-Protected Admin Portal**:
  - Sign in with username & password to view all customer inquiries in real time.
  - **Overview Metrics**: Total Leads, Most Requested Catalogue, Latest Submission Timestamp.
  - **Live Search & Filter**: Filter leads by name, email, company, city, or note.
  - **Single Lead Dossier View**: Detailed view with 1-click **Email Reply** and **WhatsApp** shortcuts.
  - **Export CSV**: 1-click download of all leads for Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Netlify Ready**: Pre-configured with `netlify.toml` and `_headers` for instant drag-and-drop hosting.
